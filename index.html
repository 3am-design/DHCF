Building a compassionate society through values, partnerships & action.

The D. H. Chen Foundation advances compassion-based philanthropy through initiatives, partnerships and sector knowledge that respond to evolving social needs.

We are a Hong Kong family philanthropic trust, putting compassion into action with partners to address evolving social needs.

Cultivating awareness of who we are

Being

Connecting the Self to the Authentic Self through mindfulness and contemplative practices

This is about presence, self-awareness, compassion, and inner wellbeing.

Growing into who we can be

Becoming

Connecting the Self to Possibility through the expression of Potential and Purpose

This is where people discover:

  • their gifts
  • their calling
  • their capacity to contribute

“Possibility” works nicely because it bridges present identity and future potential.

Recognising that we are part of something larger than ourselves

Belonging

Connecting the Self to Community through stronger Social Connections

This is where compassion extends beyond the individual and becomes relational.

The question becomes What happens when people who are grounded, purposeful, and connected decide to create change together?

That naturally leads into social innovation.

BeingBecomingBelonging

Bettering

Creating a better world together

  • Supporting social innovators through funding and proof-of-concept resources
  • Lowering barriers to entry for social impact ventures
  • Accelerating ventures towards their intended End Game and lasting impact

This feels particularly aligned with the Foundation’s philosophy because it focuses on societal improvement rather than commercial success.

Compassion is not only a value.
It is a way of working

As Visionary Founder, the Foundation launched D’Ventures, a social venture that builds a purpose-driven ecosystem for the local social good community to grow and thrive. By offering Shared Services, a Co-learning Community, and an Integrated Workspace within its D’Hub in the heart of Kowloon, the Foundation brings together the services and space needed to drive sustainable impact and turn compassion into action.

Scholarship

Stemming from a strong belief in the power of education to effect change, the Foundation established and launched the whole new The D. H. Chen Foundation Scholarship Programme (the “Scholarship Programme”) in 2015, a milestone initiative investing in deserving students who will combine their intellect and humanitarian ethos of social service to drive impact for a better tomorrow.
